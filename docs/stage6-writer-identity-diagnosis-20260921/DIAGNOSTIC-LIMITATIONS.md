# Diagnostic limits

The small identity driver used existing kernel/native waits and checked complete observer output afterward. Its Probe health callback was a no-op; it was not the frozen collector's live health integration. Heartbeat measurements are actual observer records, but this run is not a substitute for integrated medium readiness. No protected data or hash worker was involved.

The single-candidate observations remain AMBIGUOUS. They do not establish positive UNIQUE_MATCH acceptance. PID reuse did not occur in the fresh experiment, and the historical reuse record is explicitly historical only.

The bounded dequeue variant remains an experiment, not an adopted correction. It cannot solve the measured durable-flush service deficit. Byte-buffer append batching and asynchronous durable finalization have not been validated and were not introduced. No performance result here establishes a future worst-case filesystem latency.

The writer instrumentation records combined manifest generation/write/sync at finalization, rather than individually instrumenting every operation inside that one end-of-run call. No per-record manifest operation exists. Segment-size checking is part of record-validation timing; rollover decision is part of producer serialization and writer transition. Stage quantiles are estimated from an 8,192-entry reservoir. Writer lock-hold timing includes idle Monitor.Wait and must not be interpreted as exclusive lock occupancy.

The preparation failures, first failed identity run, and six failed writer trials are preserved in the evidence package. No failing run was relabelled as passing.
